TERMS & ENGAGEMENT

Terms & Conditions

Professional terms governing design consultations, project scopes, material sign-offs, and turnkey execution with Ruthvik Interior Studios.

1. Engagement & Architectural Scope

Ruthvik Interior Studios provides bespoke interior architecture, 3D visualization, material curation, custom joinery, and turnkey contracting services across Bangalore, Mysore, and Anantapur.

Every engagement begins with an exploratory design consultation. Project execution proceeds strictly based upon a mutually signed Statement of Work (SOW) or Bill of Quantities (BOQ) detailing specific deliverables, material brands, and payment milestones.

2. Design Proposals & 3D Visualizations

Preliminary sketches, concepts, and 3D renderings are provided as visual guides to represent architectural volume, lighting, and materiality.

All original design layouts, custom cabinetry details, and CAD drawings created by Ruthvik Interior Studios remain our intellectual property until formal project sign-off and complete settlement of design consulting fees.

3. Turnkey Execution & Site Conditions

Turnkey delivery timelines (typically 45–60 working days for modular interiors, or project-specific schedules for comprehensive architecture) commence once:

  • The client approves all final 2D working drawings and 3D design elevations.
  • Site possession is handed over with electrical, water, and civil readiness.
  • The stipulated milestone advance payment has been credited.

Any unforeseen site hindrances, delayed possession by developers, or external civil structural repairs may necessitate proportional adjustments to the completion schedule.

4. Material Specifications & Alterations

We adhere strictly to certified commercial materials, boiling water resistant (BWR/BWP) plywoods, European hardware fittings, and verified veneer/laminate finishes specified in your contract.

Any alterations or scope additions requested after material cutting or fabrication has commenced will be documented through formal variation orders reflecting adjusted costs and lead times.

5. Warranties & Post-Handover Care

Factory-pressed modular joinery, hardware mechanisms, and acrylic/lacquered finishes carry manufacturer-backed warranties (ranging from 5 to 10 years depending on selected tier and hardware brand). Ruthvik Interior Studios provides dedicated post-handover maintenance support during the initial warranty coverage window.

6. Governing Jurisdiction & Studio Inquiries

These terms are governed by the applicable laws of Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh, India. Any disputes arising in connection with our services shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Bangalore.
