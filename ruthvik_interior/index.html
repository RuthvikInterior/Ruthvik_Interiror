ABOUT THE STUDIO

Transforming Spaces Into Homes You Love to Live In.

At Ruthvik Interior Studios, we specialize in functional, visually refined residential interiors and end-to-end turnkey solutions. Our design approach merges practical space planning, premium materials, and photorealistic 3D visualization to deliver spaces that are as durable and intuitive to live in as they are stunning to behold.

From compact modern city apartments to expansive bespoke villas, we handle every phase of your journey under one roof:

  • Design & Visualization: Precision 2D layouts, lighting & electrical schematics, and realistic 3D room renders.
  • Custom Carpentry & Storage: Ergonomic modular kitchens, custom wardrobes, statement media consoles, and curated material finishes (veneers, acrylics, laminates, and accents).
  • Complete Turnkey Execution: Dedicated site supervision, contractor management, and quality control through to final handover.
  • Industry Collaborations: Turnkey sample flat interiors, design packages, and marketing visualizations for builders, developers, and architects.

Our Philosophy

We believe great interior design is not merely decorative—it should be functional, practical, timeless, and deeply personal. We design spaces that work effortlessly and add measurable value to your property.

WORKING METHODOLOGY

From Concept to Completion

A structured, collaborative journey delivering clarity, craftsmanship, and seamless project execution.

STAGE 1

Discovery & Consultation

We begin by understanding your property dimensions, lifestyle requirements, aesthetic sensibilities, budget scope, and functional expectations for the space.

Focus: Site assessment, functional brief & lifestyle alignment
Outcome: Clear project scope & preliminary timeline
STAGE 2

Space Planning

Developing functional 2D floor plans, circulation layouts, furniture arrangement ergonomics, and spatial zoning to optimize daylight, natural ventilation, and everyday ease.

Focus: Circulation efficiency, spatial zoning & furniture scale
Outcome: Approved 2D architectural layout & dimensional brief
STAGE 3

Design & 3D Visualisation

Curating tailored material palettes, architectural finishes, color schemes, lighting strategies, and producing photorealistic 3D interior renderings to experience your space before execution.

Focus: Material palettes, mood boards, bespoke joinery & lighting
Outcome: Realistic 3D visualizations & confirmed finish selections
STAGE 4

Execution & Coordination

Translating approved designs into physical reality through structured factory fabrication, skilled on-site carpentry, MEP coordination, false ceiling installation, and rigorous supervisor inspections.

Focus: On-site carpentry, modular joinery, false ceiling & wiring
Outcome: Precision craftsmanship adhering strictly to design drawings
STAGE 5

Final Handover

Conducting comprehensive quality audits, snag rectifications, deep site cleaning, and a complete walkthrough before handing over your beautifully finished interior space.

Focus: Detailed quality audit, hardware testing & site finishing
Outcome: Seamless move-in ready handover with documentation
